Focus the town map and use arrow keys or WASD. Press E or Enter near a location to interact. Touch users can hold the direction buttons, and keyboard users can activate them for individual steps. Water, building footprints, trees, and town bounds block movement. Releasing controls, leaving the map, navigating away, opening a dialog, or hiding the page stops movement. All seven portfolio sections remain reachable through direct links without playing.

The Poké Ball uses local Canvas rendering of a spherical surface, directional light, specular reflection, and rotation around the vertical axis. It splits into two hemispheres during entry. No external graphics library, GPU requirement, account, or paid service is needed. The original CSS ball is a fallback when Canvas is unavailable. Reduced motion renders a still sphere and skips the entrance delay; animation stops while hidden and after entry.
